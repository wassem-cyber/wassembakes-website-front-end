Privacy Policy.

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how wassembakes.com ("we," "us," or "the site"), operated by Wassem Moarsi, collects, uses, and protects information when you visit the site. By using wassembakes.com, you agree to the practices described below.

Information we collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. Depending on how you use the site, this may include:

  • Information you give us directly — for example, your email address when you subscribe to our newsletter, or your name, email, and message when you contact us.
  • Information collected automatically — such as your IP address, browser type, device, pages viewed, and how you arrived at the site. This is gathered through cookies and similar technologies (see below).

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and understand how visitors use them. We and our partners use cookies to remember preferences, measure traffic, and (where applicable) serve relevant advertising. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as intended without them.

Advertising

This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. We use these ads to help support the free content we publish.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. Google Analytics collects information such as pages visited, time on site, and general location (city/region, not precise address), using cookies. This data is aggregated and is not used to personally identify you. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Email & newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we store your email address to send you baking tips, recipes, and updates. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time. We do not sell or rent your email address to anyone.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the site and its content.
  • To send newsletters and respond to messages you send us.
  • To display advertising that helps fund the site.
  • To understand aggregate traffic and content performance.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers that make the site work — such as Google (advertising and analytics) and our email/newsletter provider — and only to the extent needed to provide those services, or when required by law.

Your choices

  • Adjust or block cookies in your browser settings.
  • Opt out of personalized ads via Google Ads Settings.
  • Unsubscribe from emails using the link in any newsletter.
  • Contact us to ask what information we hold about you, or to request its deletion.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may be noted on the site.
