Save & scale

Organize all your recipes, in your pocket.

Bake temp, bake time, ingredients by weight, your notes and a photo. Scale any batch up or down in one tap, then switch to bake mode with timers when it's time to bake.

Compare

See what changed and why it matters.

Tweak a recipe and put it next to the original. You'll know before you bake whether the change makes it denser, more crumbly or flatter, so you test less and waste fewer batches.

Ratios, cost, nutrition

Stop guessing, start building.

Exact cost, plus wholesale and retail prices, done for you. Add your ingredient prices once and every recipe shows what it costs to make and what to charge. Every ingredient is checked against ranges that work for gluten-free and vegan baking.

Wassem's bot · Premium

20 years of recipes, on call.

Muffins came out dry? Bread sank? Tell Wassem's bot what went wrong and it checks your recipe against his 20+ years of recipes, then gives you the exact fix and a jump to the screen that shows it.
